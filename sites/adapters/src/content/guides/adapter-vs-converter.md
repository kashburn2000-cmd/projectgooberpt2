If you only read one guide before your trip, make it this one. Almost every
travel-power mistake comes down to confusing two very different devices: a **plug
adapter** and a **voltage converter**. They are not interchangeable.

## A plug adapter changes shape, not electricity

A plug adapter is a passive lump of plastic and metal. All it does is let the
pins of your plug fit into a differently shaped socket. It does **nothing** to the
voltage, the current, or the frequency — the electricity flows straight through,
untouched.

You need a plug adapter whenever your plug won't physically fit the socket at your
destination. Travelling from the [United States](/country/united-states) (Type A/B)
to the [United Kingdom](/country/united-kingdom) (Type G)? The pins are completely
different, so you need an adapter. Our [route checker](/) works this out for any
country pair automatically.

## A voltage converter changes the electricity

A voltage converter (or transformer) actually changes the voltage — stepping
230&nbsp;V down to 120&nbsp;V, or the reverse. This matters because the world runs
on two broad voltage bands:

- **100–127&nbsp;V** — North America, Japan, and parts of South America
- **220–240&nbsp;V** — most of Europe, Africa, Asia, and Oceania

Plug a single-voltage 120&nbsp;V device into a 230&nbsp;V socket with only an
adapter and you can destroy it instantly — sometimes dramatically.

## The rule that keeps your devices safe

Here's the shortcut that resolves 95% of cases. **Look at the label** on your
device or its charger. If it says:

> INPUT: 100–240V, 50/60Hz

…then it is **dual-voltage** and handles any country's electricity on its own. You
only ever need a plug adapter. This covers virtually every phone charger, laptop
brick, tablet, and camera charger made in the last two decades.

If the label shows a **single** voltage (e.g. "120V" only), then it is
single-voltage. Taking it across voltage bands requires a converter — or, better,
leaving it at home and buying a dual-voltage travel version.

## Quick reference

| Situation | Adapter? | Converter? |
|---|---|---|
| Dual-voltage device, same plug type | No | No |
| Dual-voltage device, different plug type | Yes | No |
| Single-voltage device, same voltage band | Maybe (plug) | No |
| Single-voltage device, different voltage band | Yes | **Yes** |
