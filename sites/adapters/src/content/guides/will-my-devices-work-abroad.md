"Will it work over there?" The honest answer for most of your gear is: *yes, with
just a plug adapter.* But a few devices need more thought. Here's the run-down,
device by device.

## Just needs a plug adapter (dual-voltage)

These are all but universally dual-voltage (100–240V). Pack a plug adapter for your
[destination's socket type](/plug-types) and you're done:

- **Smartphones & tablets** — the charger is dual-voltage; the phone doesn't care.
- **Laptops** — that chunky brick is a universal power supply. Check it says 100–240V (it will).
- **Camera & drone battery chargers** — virtually all dual-voltage.
- **E-readers, headphones, smartwatches, power banks** — charged over USB, so whatever charges the USB adapter is all that matters.
- **Electric toothbrushes & most modern shavers** — usually dual-voltage; check the base.

## Needs care (often single-voltage)

- **Hair dryers, straighteners, curling irons** — the classic offenders. Many are single-voltage and high-wattage. Buy a dual-voltage travel model rather than lugging a converter. See our [hair-styling guide](/guides/travel-with-hair-dryer-straightener).
- **CPAP machines** — most modern units are dual-voltage and travel-friendly, but confirm on the label, and remember the humidifier may behave differently on different voltages. Bring a plug adapter and check whether your model needs distilled water rules abroad.
- **Kettles, steamers, small kitchen gadgets** — usually single-voltage; rarely worth travelling with.

## Leave it at home

- **Corded heating appliances** bought cheaply for one voltage — space heaters, irons, high-wattage kitchen devices. A converter that can handle their draw weighs more than the appliance.
- **Anything you'd be devastated to lose** if you got the voltage wrong. When in doubt, a dual-voltage travel replacement is cheap insurance.

## The universal check

Whatever the device, the workflow is always the same:

1. Read the label. `100–240V` means dual-voltage — adapter only.
2. If it's single-voltage, compare bands (is your destination 120V or 230V?).
3. Match the [plug type](/plug-types) for the country you're visiting.

You can shortcut steps 2–3 with the [route checker](/): tell it where you're going
and it returns the verdict for your devices in one line.
