## Why food comes out soggy

Crispiness is really about moisture leaving the surface faster than it builds up. An air fryer crisps by blasting hot air across food, but that only works if the air can actually reach every surface. Crowd the basket or leave food wet, and you trap steam — so it stews instead of crisping.

Nail a few basics and almost anything comes out golden.

## The real levers for crispiness

### 1. Don't overcrowd the basket

This is the big one. Food needs a single, uncrowded layer so hot air can circulate around each piece. Piling it in is the fastest way to soggy results.

- Arrange food in one layer with a little space between pieces
- Cook in batches rather than forcing one big load
- For fries or nuggets, a light overlap is fine if you shake often

### 2. Pat foods dry

Surface moisture is the enemy of crunch. Pat proteins and washed vegetables dry with a paper towel before they go in. For marinated foods, let the excess drip off first.

### 3. Use a light coat of oil

A thin film of oil helps conduct heat and brown the surface. You want *light* — a teaspoon or two tossed through, or a quick brush. Too much pools in the basket and can cause smoking. See [how much oil to use](/guides/how-much-oil-in-air-fryer) for amounts by food.

### 4. Shake, flip, or turn

Halfway through, shake the basket or flip larger pieces so every side meets the hot air. Set a timer so you don't forget — this one habit fixes a lot of pale, uneven spots.

### 5. Finish hotter

If food is cooked through but not as crisp as you'd like, bump the temperature up for the last 2–3 minutes. Most foods crisp best between 375–400°F. Watch closely at the end, since the jump from golden to burnt is quick.

## Crispiness checklist

- Pat food dry before it goes in
- Arrange a single, uncrowded layer — cook in batches if needed
- Add a light coat of oil
- Preheat the basket for crisp-focused foods
- Shake or flip halfway through
- Bump the heat for the final 2–3 minutes

A quick preheat gives food a crisp head start; if you're unsure when it's worth it, see [do you preheat an air fryer](/guides/do-you-preheat-an-air-fryer).

## Foods that reward the method

Frozen foods like [french fries](/food/frozen-french-fries) and fresh vegetables crisp beautifully when you follow the steps above. For meats, remember that crisp and *safe* aren't the same thing — cook to the right internal temperature, not just a golden color.
