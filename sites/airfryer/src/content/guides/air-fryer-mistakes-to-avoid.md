## Air fryers are forgiving — but these slip-ups cost you

Most air fryer disappointments trace back to a handful of habits. Here are the common ones and a quick fix for each.

### 1. Overcrowding the basket

Piling food in blocks airflow, so it steams instead of crisping and cooks unevenly.
**Fix:** Arrange a single, uncrowded layer and cook in batches. More in [how to get food crispy](/guides/how-to-get-food-crispy-in-air-fryer).

### 2. Not shaking or flipping

Food that never moves browns on one side and stays pale on the other.
**Fix:** Shake the basket or flip larger pieces about halfway through. Set a timer so you don't forget.

### 3. Skipping preheat when it matters

For seared meats and crisp fries, a cold start means a slow start and softer surfaces.
**Fix:** Preheat 2–5 minutes for crisp-focused foods. You can skip it for bake-style dishes — see [do you preheat an air fryer](/guides/do-you-preheat-an-air-fryer).

### 4. Using aerosol cooking spray

Aerosol sprays often contain additives that can damage the non-stick coating on many baskets over time, leaving sticky, pitted spots.
**Fix:** Toss food in a little oil, brush the basket, or use a refillable pump sprayer with plain oil instead.

### 5. Using too much oil

Air-fried food needs only a light coat. Excess pools in the basket, causes smoking, and can make food greasy rather than crisp.
**Fix:** Use a teaspoon or two, tossed through or brushed on. See [how much oil to use](/guides/how-much-oil-in-air-fryer).

### 6. Trusting the timer over a thermometer

Times are starting points that shift with wattage and basket size, and color alone won't tell you meat is safe.
**Fix:** Check meat with an instant-read thermometer and cook to the USDA safe minimum — 165°F for chicken, 160°F for ground meats, and 145°F plus a 3-minute rest for whole cuts of beef, pork, and lamb. Details in [air fryer food safety temperatures](/guides/air-fryer-food-safety-temperatures).

## Fixes at a glance

| Mistake | Quick fix |
|---|---|
| Overcrowding | Single layer, cook in batches |
| No shaking | Shake or flip halfway |
| Skipping preheat | Preheat for crisp foods |
| Aerosol spray | Brush oil or use a pump sprayer |
| Too much oil | A light teaspoon or two |
| Trusting the timer | Check internal temperature |

Avoid these six and your results get more even, crisp, and reliable almost overnight.
