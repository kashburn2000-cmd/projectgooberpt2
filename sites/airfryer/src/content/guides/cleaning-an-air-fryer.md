## Clean it often, clean it gently

A quick clean after each use keeps your air fryer working well and your food tasting fresh. The trick is to be thorough without damaging the non-stick coating on the basket and tray—that coating is what makes food release easily and cleanup fast.

The good news: it takes only a few minutes, and you don't need any special products—warm water, dish soap, and a soft sponge cover almost everything. Build the habit early and you'll never face a crust of baked-on grease.

## Let it cool first

Never clean a hot air fryer. Unplug it and give it 20 to 30 minutes to cool down. This protects your hands and keeps sudden temperature changes from warping the basket.

## The basic clean, step by step

1. **Unplug** the unit and let it cool completely.
2. **Remove** the basket and tray.
3. **Wash** them in warm, soapy water with a soft sponge or cloth. Many baskets are dishwasher-safe—check your manual to be sure before you load them.
4. **Wipe the interior** and the heating element gently with a damp cloth.
5. **Dry** every part fully before putting it back together.

## What not to do

Protecting the coating comes down to a few clear don'ts:

- **No metal utensils or tools.** Metal scratches the non-stick surface. Reach for silicone, wood, or nylon instead.
- **No abrasive scourers or steel wool.** Skip scouring pads and gritty powders; a soft sponge does the job.
- **Don't submerge the main unit.** The base holds the heating element and electronics. Wipe it with a damp cloth—never dunk it in water.
- **Be gentle around the element.** Wipe it softly once it's cool; don't scrub it or bend it.

## Stuck-on food

If something's baked on, don't attack it. Soak the basket and tray in warm, soapy water for 10 to 15 minutes to loosen the residue, then wipe it away with a soft sponge. A soft-bristled brush helps with mesh baskets. For lingering odors, a wipe-down with a little diluted vinegar, followed by a rinse, freshens things up.

## Clean between greasy batches

When you're cooking back-to-back rounds of greasy foods—wings, bacon, sausages—pause to empty and wipe the tray between batches. Pooled grease can smoke, and clearing it keeps flavors from carrying over and helps everything [crisp properly](/guides/how-to-get-food-crispy-in-air-fryer). It also means far less scrubbing at the end of the night.

A little routine care goes a long way. Keep the basket clear and the coating intact, and your air fryer will turn out clean-tasting, crispy food for years. When it's spotless and ready to go again, [browse cook times for any food](/foods) and get back to it.
