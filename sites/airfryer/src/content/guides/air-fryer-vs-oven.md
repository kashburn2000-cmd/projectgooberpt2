## They're closer than you think

An air fryer is essentially a small, fast convection oven. Both cook with moving hot air, but a full-size oven heats a large cavity while an air fryer whips air around a compact basket at high speed. That size difference drives almost every trade-off between them.

## Strengths of each machine

### Where the air fryer wins

- **Speed.** Little to no long preheat — most models are ready in a few minutes.
- **Crispness for small batches.** Tight space and fast airflow brown fries, wings, and vegetables quickly.
- **Efficiency for small jobs.** Heating a small basket beats firing up a big oven for a couple of servings.
- **Reheating.** Leftover pizza and fried foods come back crisp instead of rubbery.

### Where the oven wins

- **Capacity.** A sheet pan holds far more than a basket, so a family-size batch cooks at once.
- **Even, gentle baking.** Cakes, breads, and casseroles like a large, steady cavity.
- **Big or awkward items.** Whole roasts, multiple trays, and large dishes simply fit.
- **Hands-off cooking.** No shaking or working in batches.

## Side-by-side comparison

| Factor | Air fryer | Conventional oven |
|---|---|---|
| Preheat time | Short (2–5 min) | Longer (10–15 min) |
| Capacity | Small basket | Large, multi-tray |
| Crispiness, small batch | Excellent | Good |
| Best for | Fries, wings, veg, reheating | Roasts, baking, big batches |
| Batch cooking | Often needed | Rarely |
| Small-meal efficiency | High | Lower |

## How to choose

Reach for the air fryer when you want a few servings crisp and fast, or you're reheating and dread a soggy result. Reach for the oven when volume matters — a crowd, a big roast, or a full tray of cookies.

Times don't transfer one-to-one. Air fryers tend to run hotter and faster, so when adapting an oven recipe, drop the temperature by about 25°F and check early. The [air fryer temperature chart](/air-fryer-temperature-chart) gives solid starting points, and the crisping habits in [how to get food crispy](/guides/how-to-get-food-crispy-in-air-fryer) apply to both machines.

Whichever you use, the safety rules don't change: cook meat to its safe internal temperature and confirm with a thermometer, not the clock — see [air fryer food safety temperatures](/guides/air-fryer-food-safety-temperatures).
