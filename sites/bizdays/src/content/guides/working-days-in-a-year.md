## The short answer

A typical calendar year has about **260 to 262 weekdays** — the Mondays through Fridays that remain after you remove every Saturday and Sunday. Subtract each country's public holidays, and most places land near **250 working days** for the year.

That "~250" is a handy planning number, but treat it as an estimate. The exact count changes depending on where you are and which year you mean.

## How you get from 365 days to ~250

Here is the logic, step by step:

- Start with 365 days (366 in a leap year).
- Remove weekends. Most countries observe Saturday and Sunday, which strips out roughly 104 days.
- That leaves about **261 weekdays** in a common year.
- Remove the public holidays that fall on a weekday. If a country has 11 holidays but 2 of them land on a weekend, only 9 actually reduce your count.
- What remains is your working-day total — often somewhere between **245 and 253**.

## An illustrative breakdown

The table below shows how the same starting point produces different answers. These figures are illustrative, not official — always confirm against real per-country data.

| Step | Common year | Leap year |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Total days | 365 | 366 |
| Minus weekends (~104) | 261 | 262 |
| Minus weekday holidays (~11) | 250 | 251 |
| Approximate working days | ~250 | ~251 |

## Why the number varies

Several factors nudge the total up or down:

- **The country.** Public-holiday counts differ widely, and so does the definition of a weekend. Saturday and Sunday is common, but much of the Middle East rests Friday and Saturday — see [weekends around the world](/guides/weekends-around-the-world).
- **The year.** A leap year adds a day, and if it lands on a weekday, it adds a working day.
- **Holidays on weekends.** When a public holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, it does not reduce your weekday count — so years with "wasted" holidays have slightly more working days. Some countries grant a substitute weekday off, which cancels that effect.
- **Weekend convention.** A country that rests one day per week has far more working days than one that rests two.

## Check your exact number

Because the total shifts every year, the reliable approach is to count the real calendar rather than lean on ~250.
